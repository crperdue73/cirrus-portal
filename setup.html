Cirrus Portal

Mission control for your OpenClaw fleet.

1 Create the admin account

This is the portal's first account. There is no default login — nothing works until this is done.
Use 12+ characters with upper, lower and a number. Avoid your username.

2 Network & TLS

Where the portal listens and whether it terminates TLS. Changes to bind/port apply on the next restart.
127.0.0.1 = local only · 0.0.0.0 = all interfaces
TLS is required for a public bind. Automatic uses Caddy (see the installer's --domain); Manual means your own TLS-terminating reverse proxy. 127.0.0.1 needs no TLS.
